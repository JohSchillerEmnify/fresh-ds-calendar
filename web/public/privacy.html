Privacy policy

This Calendar instance is a private, non-commercial calendar and task-list app run for a small, invited group of people. Only Google accounts on the operator's allow-list can sign in.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account's email address and basic profile — to sign you in and check that you are on the allow-list.
  • Google Calendar — to show, create, change and delete your events, and to store your task lists in a separate calendar the app creates in your account.
  • Google Drive, app data folder only (if you allow backups) — a hidden folder that only this app can see, used to keep backups of your app settings. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.

What is stored and where

  • Your events and task lists stay in your Google Calendar.
  • App settings (theme, view preferences, task-list layout, shared lists) are stored on the app's server in the EU and, if enabled, backed up to your Google Drive app data folder.
  • A sign-in token for Google is stored on the server so the app can sync your calendar. A session cookie keeps you signed in.

What is not done

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the calendar features you see in the app. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out and revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Ask the operator (the person who invited you) to delete your app settings from the server; backups in your Drive app data folder are removed when you remove the app's access in Google Drive settings.
